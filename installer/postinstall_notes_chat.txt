Supermix Chat 74.0.0
====================

The model is inside the application. Nothing is downloaded, and the app does
not contact the network -- it starts a server on 127.0.0.1 (loopback only) on
a free port chosen at launch, and shows it in a window.

What it is good at
------------------
Arithmetic word problems, worked step by step. On a held-out benchmark of 500
freshly generated problems it scores 0.894: multiplication and division near
perfect, sequences and two-step questions ~0.98, percentages 0.75, averages
0.59.

Two things to know
------------------
1. TYPE NATURALLY. The model was trained on a specific phrasing ("What is
   47 x 6?"), and answers a differently-worded question badly. The app
   rewrites your question into that form before asking, and shows you what it
   sent as "asked as: ...". You do not need to phrase anything specially.

2. IT IS NOT A CHAT MODEL. It will answer "hello" fluently, but every
   conversational reply is reproduced word-for-word from its training data.
   Treat conversation as a demonstration, not a capability.

Answers are checked
-------------------
For question shapes it recognises, the app independently re-derives the answer
and labels the reply CORRECT or WRONG -- it will tell you when the model is
wrong rather than letting confident-looking arithmetic pass. Anything it
cannot verify is labelled NOT CHECKED, which never means correct.

The model is 8.6M parameters and runs on the CPU. A reply takes well under a
second.
